How to resize photos and signatures for a BCS application

For the BCS photo preset on this page, the target is 300×300 pixels with a maximum file size of 100 KB. The signature preset targets 300×80 pixels with a maximum file size of 60 KB.

Select the document type, upload your image, and use the tool to prepare the file. Processing is intended to happen in your browser, helping keep the image on your device instead of sending it to a HyperCompressor upload server.
