GOOD TO KNOW

Get a better result.

Export a 640×640 square. WhatsApp displays profile photos inside a circle, so keep faces and important details away from the corners. This is a practical export size, not a guarantee that WhatsApp will keep every pixel or avoid recompression.

Fit full image keeps the entire photo with a blurred or solid background. Crop mode fills a square: drag the preview and use the zoom slider or pinch with two fingers. Arrow keys also move the crop.

Enhancement uses mild level adjustment and sharpening, with optional light noise reduction. It cannot recover missing detail from a small or blurry source. Transparent areas become white. GIFs use their first frame. Browser color management converts supported CMYK images to RGB.

A few useful answers.

What is the correct WhatsApp DP size?+

This tool exports a 640×640-pixel square (1:1), a useful size for a WhatsApp profile picture. The display is circular and WhatsApp may resize or recompress the image. Keep your face near the center.

How do I set a WhatsApp DP without cropping?+

Select Fit full image and choose a blurred background or solid color. The whole photo is placed inside a square. Upload that square to WhatsApp and use its full frame. The circular display will still hide the square corners.

Why does my picture look blurry after uploading?+

A small original, heavy compression or WhatsApp’s own resizing can soften a photo. Start with a sharp original, avoid excessive zoom and use the default JPEG target. Upscaling and sharpening cannot recreate missing detail.

Are photos uploaded, and can I work offline?+

No photo is uploaded. Decoding, framing, enhancement and compression happen in this browser. Once the tool reports Ready, it works without a connection while this page stays open. Reloading offline is not supported.
