YOUR IMAGE STAYS WITH YOU

Privacy, in plain language.

Image processing

Your selected image is read into your browser and processed using HTML Canvas. The application does not send image contents or filenames to HyperCompressor servers or Google Analytics. Closing the tab releases the working image; downloaded files remain on your device.

Website delivery

Vercel hosts this website. Like other hosting providers, it receives network information such as your IP address and browser request details when you visit. This is separate from image processing.

Optional analytics

With your permission, Google Analytics receives page views and events indicating that a file was selected, processed or downloaded. Events include the tool name, but not the image or filename. Google may process network and device information and use cookies. Analytics remain off until you choose “Allow analytics”.

You can change your choice using “Cookie preferences” in the footer. Declining stops analytics collection on this site and removes the site's accessible Google Analytics cookies; it does not erase previously collected data.

Local preferences

Your theme and analytics choices are stored in your browser's local storage. They are not necessary for image processing. Clearing site data removes those preferences.

External links

Links to official portals and other websites follow those sites' privacy policies. This site does not certify or submit your application.
