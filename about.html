A LITTLE LESS FRICTION

Useful tools for everyday uploads.

HyperCompressor helps you make images fit: fewer bytes, the right dimensions and a format your form accepts.

The tools are free and process images in your browser. They are designed for students, applicants and anyone who needs to reduce an image without installing software.

What the tools do

Compress JPG and WebP, convert PNG to JPG, resize to exact pixels, and aim for a maximum file size. Results show measured output size and dimensions before you download.

What presets mean

Application presets are editable starting settings, not official requirements or a promise of acceptance. HyperCompressor is independent of the agencies named on its pages. Always consult the current instructions from the receiving portal.
