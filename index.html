GOOD TO KNOW

Get a better result.

Start with your original image. Choose JPG for photos, WebP for websites, or PNG when you need transparency.

Use a maximum file size when a form gives you a KB limit. For a required width and height, open the resize settings. Check the downloaded image before submitting it.

Read the image size guide

A few useful answers.

Are my images uploaded?+

No. Image processing happens on your device using your browser. Closing the tab releases the working image. Downloads remain wherever your browser saves them.

Will this meet every upload requirement?+

It can adjust pixels, format and file size. A portal can also check background, face position, document validity, minimum size or require live capture. Read those requirements separately.

Why did the image get smaller in pixels?+

A very small KB limit may need smaller dimensions after quality compression. Enter exact dimensions to keep them fixed; if the size limit is impossible, you will see an error instead of a misleading success.
